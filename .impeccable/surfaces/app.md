# Surface brief — app (public catalog + viewer + admin chrome)

**Mode:** Operate  
**Scope:** Full replacement visual world (notation remains ink on white paper).

## Audience & job

Musicians, students, teachers scanning a licensed score library; admins ingest metadata. Fast credit-grouped scan, open work, read/practice.

## Constraints

MusicXML canonical; no Verovio on catalog index; WCAG AA; 40/44px targets; candlelit dark mode with white notation surfaces unchanged; texture stays off engraved pages; the marbled endpaper appears only on the empty catalog and the 404.

## Direction

Archive finding-aid register as a bound index volume: aged-paper leaf, leather boards, rust rubric for live selection, Cormorant Garamond display over EB Garamond text. Standard web shell and controls; the world comes from material (laid paper, leather pore, blind tooling, marbled endpaper), type, palette, density, and extent summaries.

**Seed key:** e1c00e05 · **Form:** candidate 4 — archive finding-aid index (calf-binding recut)

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** ClearClef is a controlled archive index for engraved scores, set as a bound volume: extent lines and shelf marks before atmosphere, refusing the cream-serif "gentle library" default and card-grid streaming layouts.

**OWN-WORLD:** Aged-paper canvas (`#efe8da`), leather boards (`#241a14`), iron-gall ink, rust rubric (`#8f3418`) only for active filter/locator states and the title rule. Public Sans UI; Cormorant Garamond for display at 30px and above; EB Garamond for row titles, credit headings, running heads, shelf marks, and the wordmark, with genuine small capitals and old-style figures from the self-hosted files. Squared controls (8px radius), hairline rules, dense but legible rows. Notation frames stay pure white.

**STORY:** Visitor sees how many works sit under each credit (extent), filters with a visible "active hold" rubric, opens a score onto a white page on a muted stand; dark mode is a candlelit library, paper unchanged; with no score to show, they meet the marbled endpaper and a pasted bookplate instead of a bare message.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Leather board bar with wordmark + Catalog + theme; below, a centred title page (Cormorant H1 "Score catalog", inked rust rule, centred standfirst) over a paper filter strip; then the contents plate with a small-caps running head, small-caps credit heading + old-style extent count, rows with title, shelf line, optional preview in white mat; active filter shows a rust rule and woven ribbon marker.

**FORM:** Assigned index 4 — archive finding-aid; signature move = credit-group extent line + rust rubric and woven ribbon on active catalog filters, carried by a paper-leaf/board material system that ends the empty states on marbled endpaper.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
