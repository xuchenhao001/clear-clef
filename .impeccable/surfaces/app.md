# Surface brief — app (public catalog + viewer + admin chrome)

**Mode:** Operate
**Scope:** Full replacement visual world (notation remains ink on white paper).

## Audience & job

Musicians, students, teachers scanning a licensed score library; admins ingest metadata. Fast credit-grouped scan, open work, read/practice.

## Constraints

MusicXML canonical; no Verovio on catalog index; WCAG AA; 40/44px targets; near-black night mode with white notation surfaces unchanged; texture stays off engraved pages; the marbled endpaper appears only on the empty catalog and the 404.

## Direction

Frontier ledger taken from the Red Dead Redemption menus: near-black hide panels carrying the content on an aged map-paper floor, brass for every rule and ornament, vermilion for what is live only, heavy slab capitals over an old-style reading serif. The world comes from the game's own value structure (dark panels, light type — the paper is the floor between them), material (embossed, raking-lit hide; brass-tooled seams; marbled endpaper), type, palette and extent summaries. No sans-serif, no small capitals, no third display face.

**Seed key:** e1c00e05 · **Form:** candidate 4 — archive finding-aid index (Red Dead recut)

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** ClearClef is a controlled ledger index for engraved scores: extent lines and shelf marks before atmosphere, refusing the cream-serif "gentle library" default and card-grid streaming layouts. The frontier world is carried by hide, brass, vermilion and heavy slab, not by a pastel wash — and the value structure is the game's, dark ground with light type, never a pale card.

**OWN-WORLD:** Map-paper floor (`#cdb79a`), near-black hide panels (`#16110e` / `#1a1513` at night), board ink `#f0ece4`, board muted `#b3a08c`, panel hairline `#3a302b`, brass (`#a5751a` on paper, `#f2a92c` on hide) for the title rule, plate edge, closing star, board seam, focus ring and the current-tab underline, vermilion (`#d92b1e`) for state alone, slate (`#59616e`) for cool metadata and newsprint olive (`#414a3f`) for printed marks. One rule governs type: **capitals = Bitter slab (heavy, tightly tracked), sentence case = EB Garamond**, technical strings in Geist Mono. Squared controls (8px), hairline rules, notation frames pure white on a dark hide mat.

**STORY:** Visitor sees how many works sit under each credit (extent), filters with a visible vermilion dot on a held value, opens a score onto a white page on dark hide; night mode is the menu itself, paper unchanged; with no score to show they meet the marbled endpaper.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Near-black hide bar with the brass-swept slab wordmark, slab caps Catalog + Admin + theme, the current tab underlined in **brass**; below, a hide title plate (slab caps "Score catalog" at weight 900, brass rule, standfirst) then a hide filter strip and a hide contents plate with a brass top edge, a caps running head, a serif credit heading with an olive extent count, rows with serif titles, olive shelf lines and a white preview; the map paper shows only in the gutters between panels. An active filter shows a rubric rule and the one vermilion dot.

**FORM:** Assigned index 4 — archive finding-aid; signature move = credit-group extent line + a vermilion dot on live filters, carried by a lit hide/paper material system that ends the empty states on marbled endpaper.
